# Mechanical prototype

The team reports a printed catamaran with two angular pontoons, central support and electronics enclosure, two rear propulsion motors/propellers, a rear rudder and servo, battery holder and wiring.

The intended propellers operate below the waterline and produce horizontal thrust. The vessel remains at the surface.

This directory currently contains documentation only. Editable CAD, STL/STEP exports, measured dimensions, component-fit evidence and sealing/buoyancy results have not been committed here. The [Three.js visualization](../prototype/README.md) approximates six reference photographs; it is not manufacturing CAD.

Before water testing: document assembly fit, mass/balance, sealing, safe rudder travel, propulsion mounting and a controlled test procedure. Interchangeable-payload performance is a future concept, not a verified result.
