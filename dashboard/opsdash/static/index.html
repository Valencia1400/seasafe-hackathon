    How to read this dashboard

    Mission: READY → RUNNING → STOPPED → (Reset) READY. An emergency stop or a fault gives FAULT; only a confirmed Reset returns to READY, and Reset never restarts the motor.

    Safety: the real ESP32 must stop the motor on its own; the laptop is never the only safety mechanism. Communication loss (simulated): if the control ESP32 hears no laptop heartbeat for 1.0 s it stops the motor itself (COMMLOST). The dashboard shows the link as lost after 1.0 s without telemetry.

    Camera: object detection only. Potential objects for operator review; not an assessment of harbour damage or of any emergency. The camera is not part of the motor safety chain, and detections never control the motor or rudder.

    Rudder: a starboard (+) rudder swings the stern to port and turns the bow to starboard. Demo order: start and stop, tilt, cut comms then wait about 1 s, restore and reset, emergency stop and reset, then the rudder buttons. "Restart simulation" resets the demo harness; it is not a safety reset.

    Software simulation only, apart from the camera. Nothing here proves physical safety: motor safety must be enforced independently by the control ESP32. PID steering and hardware mode are future work.
