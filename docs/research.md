# Problem evidence and user validation

SeaGuardian targets preliminary water-level observations for harbour maintenance and operations personnel following storms or incidents. Operators would review observations to prioritize specialist inspection; the product does not certify infrastructure condition.

## Sources supplied by the team

- [SMHI: Storm Babet, October 2023](https://www.smhi.se/kunskapsbanken/oceanografi/hogvattenhandelser-i-sverige/stormflod-under-babet-oktober-2023): Swedish coastal storm-flood context. The handover reports 126 cm above mean sea level at Simrishamn; verify the exact statistic before using it in publication.
- [SVT: Borgholm harbour storm damage](https://www.svt.se/nyheter/lokalt/smaland/stormskador-for-miljoner-pa-borgholms-hamnar-reparationer-klara-till-sommaren): additional problem context supplied by the team.

These links were carried forward from the team handover, not independently reverified during this documentation cleanup. Storm damage establishes context, not SeaGuardian's effectiveness or savings.
