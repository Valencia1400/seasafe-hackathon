# Pitch outline

- **Problem:** harbour operators need preliminary information after storms or incidents. Use sourced Swedish context and acknowledge missing direct operator validation.
- **Concept:** a compact remotely supervised surface catamaran combining camera observations, object detection, telemetry and operator controls.
- **Evidence:** show the physical prototype, current software and appropriately labelled tests. See the [evidence table](../tests/test-results.md).
- **AI engineering:** demonstrate a documented failed assumption, human diagnosis, correction and retest from the [AI log](../docs/ai-process.md).
- **Next step:** reconcile the electronics builds, verify physical safety/control behaviour, then controlled water trials and harbour-operator feedback.

Do not claim autonomous navigation, structural damage detection or demonstrated cost/time savings. Confirm presentation timing with the organizers.
